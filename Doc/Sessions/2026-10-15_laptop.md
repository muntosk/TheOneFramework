## What was asked, decided and built (in order)

1. **Read the docs.** Narrative, structure, UF rubric read. Notable rubric gaps found then: progress HUD,
   multiple scenes, player mesh (not the default robot), hierarchy labels, animation events/curves,
   terrain requirements, lighting extras, particles changed by script.
2. **Start menu / "HUD with start level".** The teacher's framework has no menu system (`LoadScene`/`ShowMenu`
   are player-triggered `PlayerActivatable`s), so a custom `MainMenu` + editor builder was made, reusing
   `VolumeSlider`/`GameAudio` (rubric: mixer + settings UI) and the Titillium font. First Level is a scene
   field (drag a scene in; it's added to the build list automatically).

4. **Water kills + respawn.** `KillZone` + `RespawnBehaviour.Kill()`: fade out, respawn on the last *safe ground*

   level start.
5. **Seamless lift.** Next level preloads while the lift rides; screen fades during the last second; arrival
   lift fades back in. `ScreenFade` survives scene loads.
6. **Level kit as the main way to build.** Decision: repeatable blocks go in `PortalLevelKitBuilder`; hand-made
   prefabs (lift, door, buttons, dispenser + variants) stay hand-made (good for the prefab-variant criteria).
   Never hand-edit generated kit prefabs.
7. **ToxicWater** in the kit — first Portal goo, then (user's wish) the URP water material (`Water1`) as a flat
   surface + 1 m KillZone trigger. Tool: Replace Water With ToxicWater (open scenes).
8. **Bug: dying 2 s into the arrival lift.** The replaced water plane was 73×76 m and covered the lift shaft.
   Fixes: KillZone only kills when you fell in *from above*; riders of a moving lift are immune.
   Advice: scale water to the pits only.

   in the unityhub FHS sandbox → python3 added via `extraPkgs` in `/etc/nixos/modules/home/apps.nix`.
   Registered as `unityMCP` (HTTP :8080).
10. **Level_Test = Chamber 1 "Welcome"** built live (portals intro, goo pit, ledge, plate → exit door,
    S.A.F.E.-T lines, security camera).
11. **Bug: couldn't walk through a wall portal.** White panels were mounted in front of black walls; portals
    only ignored the panel. Fix in `Portal.cs`: travellers ignore every solid collider right behind a portal.
12. **Chamber prefab** (user's idea): lifts + corridor already connected, only the room size changes per level.
    `ChamberShell` (size in panels, openings, optional floor, walls below floor for pits) +
